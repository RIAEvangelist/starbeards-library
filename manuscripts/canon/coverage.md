# Ten-story character coverage

The character-writing team read the complete ten stories, the Pigeon series notes, and the applicable illustration records. Each assigned reader authored and reread their supporting records. The website owner reconciled the core cast and reviewed the complete four character inventories. This records literary source coverage, not a claim of automated testing or exhaustive browser execution.

## Unnamed and passing participants

Distinct unnamed participants with sustained actions receive their own descriptive profiles. The nine origin profiles are Apricot's captain, Lantern's captain, the paper-bird passenger, the young beetle pilot, the independent cruiser captain, the Bellhouse worker leader, the tortoise governor, the Old Wing cavern keeper and the storm-warning messenger.

Three origin community profiles preserve the brief roles chapter by chapter. They include remembered relatives and acquaintances; recruiting and receiving officers; additional rescuers, residents and youngsters; healers and keepers; shuttle and patrol pilots; deck, cargo and clinic crews; a taller passenger of unspecified relationship; Mallow workers and an unidentified sleeping captain; survey and school-ferry groups; flight leaders, scouts, medical staff, tugs, gate workers and recovery crew; the flagship navigator; commanders and envoys; children, photographers and the echo-arguing pigeon. Each record keeps possible identity overlaps open. A sleeping convoy captain does not create a fourth ship's captain.

Other ensembles retain shared identities when the source treats them collectively: nestmates, stars, moonlets, singing plants, Melodians' flowers, Lumi, crystal creatures and academy gardeners. The three individually distinguishable Melodians have separate pages. The Space Samurai entry covers the named tradition without inventing another onstage member.

## Additions, objects and uncertainties

Every profile includes new fictional backstory in its `backstory` field, shown under **Beyond the pages**. These additions establish useful personal habits, community practices or compatible earlier experience. They remain distinct from the source-grounded `established` field. The Sock Thief's addition remains an in-world legend because the story only reports the earlier encounter.

Existing objects keep their original roles. Ships, the paper bird, route cloth, dream seed, treasure crystals and stuffed toys are not given living-character identities. The dragon-shaped rock in Crystal Cave is a landmark. “Static Cling” is humorous personification. Artwork's unsupported memory figures do not become newly documented encounters. The illustrated pearl-bubble nestmates are explicitly included as visual supporting beings whose kinship is unknown.

Corrections and unresolved differences are recorded in [world and chronology](world-and-chronology.md) and the individual profile notes: Pigeon's anatomy and coos; Vela's Captain rank; the first meeting and Pip's awakening/naming; corrected scarf and lamp staging; Starbeard's contextual titles; separate ship identities; Uni's story-specific mane; three distinct elders; the Heart's spherical prose description; Naptune's awake crescent and three-petaled gift; and the newly adopted golden-eldest Melodian assignment.

The full Pluto manuscript supplies details omitted by the shorter web template, including the snowdrift bow, three Lumi riding Uni's mane and Estrella's starry midnight skin. These remain available to the character guide. Original story templates and manuscripts were preserved in this increment. Private photographic references and real family biography were excluded from the fictional profiles.
