# Starbeard’s Library — shared canon

This is the shared editorial home for the ten existing stories and their comic adaptations. Original story manuscripts and the complete library templates remain unchanged. Later direct author corrections take precedence over earlier prose drafts or generated art. New profile backstories will be identified separately from events established in those sources.

## Initial continuity baseline

The full character and world inventory is in progress. Use the following source-grounded baseline immediately; faithful adaptations need not wait for the directory.

- Read `../admiral-pigeon-origins/series-notes.md` and its `README.md` with each relevant manuscript. Their chronology is: the three origin books, then **The Runaway Cup**, then a long time later **The Great Galactic Sock Caper**. No exact ages or calendar years are established.
- Pigeon is an ordinary small gray pigeon with a turquoise-and-violet iridescent neck, natural bird anatomy, a dark charcoal beak and a pale cere. He wears no clothes or hat. Rank insignia belong on perch plates or cabin doors. His conversations are coos interpreted by the narrator or his companions.
- Mother Tansy, Father Bram and Gran Cora remain alive. The family route cloth is a practical inherited map, never a prophecy or universal magic answer. Three steps, stop, look back means checking that a companion can follow.
- Pigeon qualifies as an Airforce flyer in origin Book One, becomes Flight Lieutenant after Book Two’s convoy rescue, and earns Admiral after Book Three’s battle. The formal organization spelling is **Galactic Pigean Space Corps**. Captain Vela’s rank is Captain even where older illustration prompts say Commander.
- Pigeon’s first assignment as a new admiral is Starling Academy on Luma in the Prism Galaxy. He has not yet met young Captain Star Beard. Their first meeting and the captain’s first voyage in an unnamed secondhand blue-and-brass ship happen in **The Runaway Cup**.
- At the end of the trilogy, the turquoise magical AI cup is still being built and is unnamed and asleep. It has gold circuit filigree, one golden handle, two brass legs and no arms. It wakes in **The Runaway Cup**, then chooses the name **Pip** after the rescue.
- Natural pigeon anatomy and the cup’s later corrected design override conflicting older images. Preserve source-era costumes for other characters unless a later direct correction applies.
- Preserve each story’s spelling of Starbeard / Star Beard and its contextual Captain / Admiral title. Admiral Pigeon is a separate character. The prequel explicitly connects the young captain with the later purple-sock adventure; identity must follow that evidence rather than name resemblance alone.
- Preserve the named ships in their own stories. Moonlit’s *Cosmic Wanderer*, Sock Caper’s *Starlight Wanderer*, and the young captain’s unnamed ship have no established renaming or replacement history.
- Luma, the academy moon, is not established as Luma Bloom, the planet; Lumi names a people. Do not merge them.
- Keep real family biography and private photographic references out of public character profiles. These are fictional story histories.

## Working authority

The character website task owns this directory and shared website source. Comic tasks own only their assigned ignored `output/comics/<slug>/` folder. Send specific contradictions and proposed additions to the canon owner; do not edit another task’s output. Full comic editions remain local unless separately authorized for publication.

Source references and the full inventory will be recorded here as the complete-story review is reconciled. Absence from this initial list is not permission to invent a previously named character or merge unnamed roles.
