# The Adventures of Starbeard and Uni

## Book 3: The Treasure of Pluto and the Luminous Labyrinth

Adapted for the illustrated web reader from the supplied Book 3 manuscript.

### Spread 1 — Cover

- Label: `Volume Three`
- Heading: `The Treasure of Pluto and the Luminous Labyrinth`
- Artwork: `assets/book-pluto-labyrinth-cover.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard and Uni sail toward frozen Pluto while a golden maze glows among the stars.`

Starbeard and his best friend Uni travel to frozen Pluto in search of diamonds and rubies. But beyond the treasure wait new friends, a doorway in space, and a golden maze filled with memories.

### Spread 2 — The Asteroids

- Label: `The asteroids`
- Heading: `Pluto was waiting beyond the asteroids.`
- Artwork: `assets/pluto-asteroids.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard steers through an asteroid field while Uni bounces through the colorful starship bridge.`

Far beyond the familiar planets, Starbeard's ship floated beside an enormous pink nebula.

Starbeard was not a pirate. He was the captain of a starship—and somewhere beneath Pluto's frozen surface waited diamonds and rubies.

Starbeard checked the coordinates. Uni checked the snack cabinet. Then she checked it again.

“Engines ready.”

**WHOOOOOOOM!**

Stars stretched into silver ribbons. Then the alarms cried **BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!** Asteroids tumbled everywhere.

Starbeard dipped beneath one rock, twisted around another, and squeezed between two enormous boulders with barely a moonbeam to spare. Uni bounced around the bridge, her purple mane standing straight up.

At last, they burst into clear space.

“UNICORN!” Uni cheered.

Starbeard laughed—and noticed the mysterious little animal clinging to his chair.

“Oh! Right! You're here too!”

Five minutes later, they forgot again.

### Spread 3 — Pluto

- Label: `Pluto`
- Heading: `Beneath the blue ice waited a treasure—and a teacher.`
- Artwork: `assets/pluto-treasure.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard, Uni, and a robed Sensei discover a chest of diamonds and rubies beneath Pluto's ice.`

Their boots and hooves crunched across Pluto's glittering blue ice. Ahead stood a robed stranger carrying a sword made of light.

“I am the Sensei,” he said with a bow.

Uni bowed so deeply that she rolled forward into a snowdrift.

The Sensei taught Starbeard the ancient ways of the Space Samurai: how to stand, how to move, how to watch, and how to know when to fight—and when not to.

Together they followed a trail through the frozen caverns. When shadowy crystal creatures blocked the way, Starbeard remembered the lesson. He lowered his glowing sword, listened to their warning hum, and found a peaceful path around them.

At the cavern's end, a doorway waited beneath the ice.

Beyond it sparkled thousands of diamonds and brilliant red rubies.

“We found it,” Starbeard whispered.

They filled a treasure chest. Yet as Uni danced among the gems, Starbeard knew the finest part of the journey was exploring the cosmos with his best friend.

### Spread 4 — The Lumi

- Label: `The alien planet`
- Heading: `The strange signal was an invitation.`
- Artwork: `assets/lumi-festival.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard and Uni dance with rainbow Lumi beside a river of starlight.`

After Pluto, a strange signal blinked across the starship controls.

It led them to a planet that was not on any map. Blue plants glowed beside rivers of liquid starlight.

Something rustled.

**POP!**

A tiny rainbow creature appeared.

Then another.

Then twenty more.

“Greetings!” they chimed. “We are the Lumi!”

The signal was not a warning. It was an invitation to their Festival of Light.

That evening, thousands of Lumi gathered beneath ribbons of pink, blue, green, and gold. They sang. They danced. Uni danced particularly enthusiastically. She spun so fast that three Lumi used her flowing mane as a merry-go-round.

Before the explorers left, a gentle Lumi elder gave them a cluster of glowing crystals.

“These will help guide you on journeys yet to come.”

It was another treasure—but this one was a gift from friends.

### Spread 5 — The Asteroid Station

- Label: `The asteroid station`
- Heading: `Their fuel was low, but Zogo had a plan.`
- Artwork: `assets/zogo-station.png`
- Alt text: `A cheerful green alien named Zogo welcomes Starbeard and Uni with a basket of blue fuel crystals.`

Soon the fuel gauge fell from half full… to quarter full… to dangerously low.

Behind a giant asteroid, Uni spotted a tiny brass-and-glass station. Its owner was a cheerful green alien named Zogo.

“I can refuel your ship!” Zogo said. “But the special blue crystals are trapped among the asteroids.”

So Starbeard and Uni climbed into a little shuttle.

Starbeard piloted between the tumbling rocks. Uni's magical horn shone like a lantern, revealing one hidden crystal after another.

“Left!” cried Starbeard.

“UNICORN!” cried Uni, which meant exactly the same thing—only louder.

Back at the station, Zogo placed the crystals inside the engines.

**WHOOOOOOOM!**

The whole ship glowed blue.

“Full tank!” Zogo announced. He gave them one small humming crystal as a souvenir, and off they went again.

### Spread 6 — The Cosmic Café

- Label: `The cosmic café`
- Heading: `Even great explorers need sparkling star juice.`
- Artwork: `assets/cosmic-cafe.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard, Uni, and their mysterious tiny companion sip sparkling drinks in a café among the stars.`

Eventually, even great explorers need a break.

And Uni was thirsty.

Fortunately, floating nearby was a café in space. Inside were enormous windows, pink couches, blue chairs, purple lights, and drinks that sparkled.

Starbeard and Uni sipped star juice and talked about Pluto, the Sensei, the Lumi, and Zogo.

Then Starbeard frowned.

“Didn't someone else come with us?”

Uni tilted her head.

“Unicorn?”

They both shrugged.

Beside their table, the mysterious little animal lowered its third straw and sighed.

Starbeard finally looked down. “Oh! There you are!”

The animal brightened.

Somehow, five minutes later, they forgot about it again.

### Spread 7 — The Celestial Isles

- Label: `The Celestial Isles`
- Heading: `A doorway in space opened onto islands of light.`
- Artwork: `assets/celestial-isles.png`
- Alt text: `Estrella guides Starbeard and Uni across a bridge of light toward floating celestial islands.`

After the café, the Lumi crystals began to glow. Their light joined into a map, and the map led to an enormous doorway floating in space.

Beyond it were islands among clouds, glowing mushrooms, rivers of stardust, and bridges made entirely of light.

A tall guardian named Estrella welcomed them to the Celestial Isles. Her midnight skin shimmered with tiny stars.

She guided Starbeard and Uni along a golden bridge to an enormous glowing sphere called the Heart of the Isles.

When Uni touched the Heart with her horn, pictures appeared all around them: Pluto, the treasure, the Lumi, Zogo, and every adventure they had shared.

Then the crystal map glowed again.

A new destination appeared in golden light:

**THE LUMINOUS LABYRINTH.**

### Spread 8 — The Luminous Labyrinth

- Label: `The Luminous Labyrinth`
- Heading: `The maze remembered every step of their journey.`
- Artwork: `assets/luminous-labyrinth.png`
- Alt text: `Uni reveals a glowing path while Starbeard holds the star crystal at the center of a golden cosmic maze.`

The labyrinth floated in space, its golden walls twisting through pink, purple, and blue stardust.

Inside, left became right. Right became left. Doors appeared and disappeared. Plants changed color, and crystals hummed little songs.

In a hall of mirrors, Starbeard and Uni saw memories instead of reflections: Pluto's blue ice, the Lumi festival, Zogo's smile, and Estrella's shining bridge.

At last, every passage curled back to the same quiet pool.

Uni dipped one hoof into the stardust.

**PLOOP.**

Golden symbols rose from the ripples and revealed the correct path.

At the maze's center, a single star-shaped crystal floated in the air. It settled gently into Starbeard's hands, and the entire labyrinth shone brighter.

They had completed the maze.

Now it was time to go home.

### Spread 9 — Home Base

- Label: `Home base`
- Heading: `The finest treasure was a warm place to share it.`
- Artwork: `assets/home-base-treasures.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard and a yawning Uni cuddle by the fire among the glowing treasures from their journey.`

At home, Starbeard unpacked the diamonds of Pluto, the red rubies, the color-changing Lumi crystals, Zogo's humming fuel crystal, and the star crystal from the labyrinth.

“That's quite a collection.”

Uni gave a gigantic unicorn yawn.

Soon they were wrapped in a thick stitched blanket beside the fireplace, with hot cocoa and marshmallows warming their paws and hands.

Their treasures glowed softly around them—blue, red, rainbow, and gold.

Nearby, the mysterious little animal was already asleep on a cushion.

“Oh,” Starbeard whispered. “There you are.”

The animal opened one hopeful eye.

Then Starbeard forgot again.

The fire crackled.

**Pop. Crackle. Pop.**

Starbeard and Uni closed their eyes.

### Spread 10 — The Shared Dream

- Label: `The shared dream`
- Heading: `There would always be more stars tomorrow.`
- Artwork: `assets/shared-dream.png`
- Alt text: `Starbeard and Uni dream of a cosmic elder offering them two tiny stars beside a stardust castle.`

That night, Starbeard and Uni dreamed the same dream.

They floated along a river of starlight to a castle built from stardust. Inside, an ancient cosmic elder with galaxies in his eyes showed them moving pictures of their adventures.

“You have collected many treasures,” he said. “But these are treasures too.”

Their memories.

Their friends.

And each other.

The elder gave Starbeard and Uni one tiny star each.

“When you wake, they will point toward your next journey.”

Then the dream softened, and they drifted home again. Beside the fireplace, the labyrinth crystal gave one quiet pulse.

**Glow.**

Outside, shooting stars crossed the sky as the whole universe seemed to whisper:

**Good night, Starbeard.**  
**Good night, Uni.**  
**Sleep well, little explorers.**

There would always be another adventure.

But for now, it was time to dream.

**The end… for tonight.**
