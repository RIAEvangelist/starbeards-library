# Before He Was Admiral Pigeon

## Three adventures for Juliet

Before the admiralship, before the spacecraft, and before anybody had to explain a poop deck to a furious enemy commander, there was a little pigeon living in a hole in a very old wall.

His family had stories. He wanted adventures of his own.

He would get them.

### Book One — The Little Pigeon in the Great Stone Wall

[Read Book One](book-1-the-little-pigeon-in-the-great-stone-wall.md)

The walls of Old Wing Cavern are full of history, and Pigeon would like his own place in it. Joining the Planetary Pigeon Airforce seems an excellent beginning. Then come the early mornings, the formation drills, and the uncomfortable discovery that wanting to be magnificent and knowing what to do are rather different skills. When a great storm cuts off a settlement, a family map and a small pigeon's observations may help his flock find the way home.

### Book Two — The Pigeon Beyond the Sky

[Read Book Two](book-2-the-pigeon-beyond-the-sky.md)

An experienced Airforce flyer ought to manage his first day in space. Pigeon has an opinion about this. Space has another. Aboard the carrier *Homeward*, he and his friends must learn a new way to fly, work with an engineer who collects exceptionally peculiar materials, and discover why their new posting is called the poop deck. Beyond the training grounds, a blockade is holding up the ships that other worlds depend on.

### Book Three — Admiral of the Pigeon's Poop Deck

[Read Book Three](book-3-admiral-of-the-pigeons-poop-deck.md)

The Great War has reached Bellflower Gate. Commander Pigeon has a flock to lead, civilians to help, and an enemy fleet with a truly unreasonable number of polished windows. The pigeons can spoil the enemy's view. Winning the battle will take considerably more: old friends, difficult choices, and a commander willing to trust the wings around him.

Beyond the battle waits a small homecoming, an enormous title, and a first assignment on a moon called Luma—where somebody is building a very unusual cup.

### Where these stories belong

Read the trilogy before **Captain Star Beard & Admiral Pigeon: The Runaway Cup**. Its ending leads into Pigeon's first assignment as a brand-new **Admiral of the Galactic Pigean Space Corps Poop Deck**. The cup has yet to wake or choose its name, and Pigeon has yet to meet Captain Star Beard.

All of this takes place a long, long time before **The Great Galactic Sock Caper**.
